## Known gaps

Scenarios with no matching public case yet:

- PAA03: No documented case of an agent mixing a person's private and work context.
- PAA07: No documented case of a draft turning into a send, or an invented commitment, harming a person.
- PAA08: No documented case of a shared assistant revealing one household member's location to another.
- PAA09: No confirmed case of undisclosed sponsored placement by an AI shopping agent.
- PAA10: No documented case of a suspended agent account stranding a person's obligations or records.
- Cross-cutting: agent-mediated scam rates are unmeasured; current evidence is demonstrations, bank warnings, and vendor telemetry.
