The Top 10 risks of personal AI agents

When an agent can read your mail, remember your life, spend your money and speak in your name, ordinary software failures become personal ones. PAA10 names ten ways that delegation goes wrong, who must fix each, and how to test it.

How each entry works

Every risk pairs a decision, a duty and a test

Built on the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications layout, and extended for people who run their agent without a security team.

A decision for the person

Plain controls you can use: narrow, pause or end a delegation, preview what is sent, keep recovery separate from the agent.

A duty for the provider

The layer that must enforce it: agent service, integration or account provider. Enforced outside the model, not by a prompt.

A test anyone can repeat

Synthetic data, observable effects, a clear pass or fail. No real money, live credentials or other people's data.

Crosswalk

Reuses existing OWASP mechanisms, adds the personal harm

PAA10 does not claim new attack classes. It maps to the Agentic Top 10 (ASI) and the LLM Top 10, and adds who is harmed and what ordinary people can do.
